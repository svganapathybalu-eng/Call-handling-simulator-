5 Stages Coaching Cheat Sheet

Stage 1: Call Opening Seconds 0–5

Greeting + Brand Name + Your Name + Assistance Offer.

"Thank you for calling Apex Wireless, my name is Alex. How may I assist you today?"

Stage 2: Rapport & Empathy De-escalation

Acknowledge emotion, express sincere empathy without arguing.

"I completely understand how frustrating that delay is, Marcus. If I were in your position, I would feel the exact same way. Let's fix this right now."

Stage 3: Identifying Needs Probing

Ask open probing questions, verify identity and order specifics.

"Could you please confirm your account number and explain what the tracking status indicates?"

Stage 4: Problem Solving & Protocols Strict Compliance

  • Hold Protocol: Ask permission + state clear reason + time estimate (1-2 mins) + thank upon return.
  • Transfer Protocol: Explain why + name department + warm handover.

"May I place you on a brief 1 to 2 minute hold while I contact the dispatch team?"

Stage 5: Call Closing Final Impression

Summarize resolution + ask "anything else?" + brand farewell.

"I have processed your expedited replacement. Is there anything else I can assist you with today? Thank you for choosing Apex, have a wonderful day!"
