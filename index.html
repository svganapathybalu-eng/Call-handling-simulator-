5 Stages Call Handling Rubric

Stage 1: Call Opening Seconds 0–5

Company Greeting + Agent Name + Help Offer.

"Thank you for calling Apex Financial, my name is Alex. How may I assist you today?"

Stage 2: Rapport & Empathy De-escalation

Acknowledge distress sincerely. Never invalidate or talk over them.

"I completely understand how stressful this unauthorized charge is, Victoria. You have reached the right person, and I will personally secure this for you."

Stage 3: Identifying Needs Probing

Ask open probing questions and verify account credentials.

"To protect your privacy, could you please confirm the last 4 digits of the card and describe the unauthorized transaction amount?"

Stage 4: Issue Resolution & Etiquette Protocol Critical

  • Hold Protocol: Ask permission + state time duration (1-2 mins) + thank upon return.
  • Transfer Protocol: Explain necessity + introduce department + warm handover.

"May I place you on a brief 1 to 2 minute hold while I contact our Fraud Prevention desk to stop the transaction?"

Stage 5: Call Closing Final CSAT

Summarize actions + check if anything else is needed + company signoff.

"I have blocked the card and initiated the refund. Is there anything else I can assist you with today? Thank you for calling Apex, have a wonderful rest of your day!"
